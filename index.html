About Us

Welcome to Kerala Cafe — your gateway to the authentic flavours and vibrant culture of Kerala. Our recipes are deeply rooted in tradition, crafted with the freshest ingredients and cooked with genuine passion.

From the backwaters of Alleppey to the heart of Swansea, we bring you the very best of South Indian home cooking. Whether it is a fragrant Malabar curry, crispy dosas, or fresh-caught seafood prepared in the Kerala style, every dish tells a story.

Join us for a dining experience that goes beyond a meal — a journey into one of India's most celebrated culinary traditions.
