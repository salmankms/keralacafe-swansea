Our Story

Kerala Cafe Restaurant

Kerala Cafe was founded with one simple ambition: to share the authentic flavours of South India with the people of Swansea. Our chefs bring decades of experience and deep-rooted family recipes that have been passed down through generations.

We source the freshest ingredients — coconut milk pressed daily, spices ground in-house, and seafood delivered fresh each morning. Every dish tells a story of Kerala's rich culinary heritage, from the fragrant rice paddies of Palakkad to the spice-laden markets of Kozhikode.

From the backwaters of Alleppey to the streets of Swansea, Kerala Cafe is your bridge to one of India's most celebrated food cultures. Whether you are a long-time lover of Indian cuisine or trying it for the very first time, we promise a warm welcome and an unforgettable meal.
